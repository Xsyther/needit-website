How it works

Three steps. That's it.

No algorithms deciding what you see. No influencers paid to pretend they love something. Just your need, and brands fighting to solve it.

01 ✍️

Post your real need

Write exactly what you're looking for — be specific. The more detail you give, the better brands can respond. Include budget, use case, location — whatever matters.

02 🏆

Brands compete to help you

Subscribed brands see your post and respond with why their product fits your need best. They're competing for your attention — not the other way around.

03 🗳️

Community votes on the best answer

Other users vote on the most helpful brand response. The best answers rise to the top. Brands with the best responses build real trust — not fake follower counts.
